## Conclusions
- Booking count reached maximum in fall season
- Booking count was minimum in spring season
- Bookings kept increasing month over month, reached peak around jun – sept, then started decreasing; roughly like a bell curve
- Maximum bookings happened on Saturday, the amount dipped on Sunday, and then kept slightly increasing over the days
- Booking demand seemed to decrease on holidays and increase on working days
- Booking demands were maximum in clear weather. Demands decreased on mist, even lesser in light_snow and practically none in heavy_rain
- Booking demand increased by a very big margin from 2018 to 2019
- Booking demand has highest correlation to temperature
- Booking demand is mainly influenced by year, temperature, windspeed, winter, jan, sept, sunday, light_snow, mist, summer
